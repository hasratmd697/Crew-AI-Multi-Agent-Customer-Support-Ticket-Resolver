# Promotions & Coupon Terms
## Section 10.1 - Coupon Stacking
Unless explicitly stated otherwise, promotional codes and coupons cannot be combined or "stacked" on a single order. Only one discount code may be applied per checkout.

## Section 10.2 - Post-Purchase Price Adjustments
We do not apply coupons retroactively. If a customer forgets to apply a coupon code at checkout, we cannot refund the difference or apply the code after the order has been placed.

## Section 10.3 - Refunds Involving Coupons
When an item purchased with a promotional discount is returned, the refund amount will be the actual price paid after the discount was applied, not the retail value of the item.

## Section 10.4 - Free Gift Promotions
If an order includes a "Free Gift with Purchase" and the qualifying item is returned, the free gift must also be returned in unused condition. If the free gift is not returned, its retail value will be deducted from the refund amount.
