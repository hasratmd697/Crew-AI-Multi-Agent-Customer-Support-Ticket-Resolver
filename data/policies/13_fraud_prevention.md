# Fraud Prevention & Account Security
## Section 13.1 - Concession Abuse Limits
We actively monitor accounts for excessively high return rates, frequent claims of "lost in transit," or repetitive damaged item claims. Accounts exceeding our internal concession limits will be flagged by our fraud systems. 

## Section 13.2 - Return Bans
Customers with flagged accounts will lose the privilege of returning items and receiving refunds for lost packages. In such cases, the customer will be notified via email that their return privileges have been revoked.

## Section 13.3 - Payment Method Verification
For high-value orders (exceeding $1,500), we may require additional verification of the payment method before the order is processed. If verification is not provided within 48 hours, the order will be cancelled.
