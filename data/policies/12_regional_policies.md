# Regional Policy Variations
## Section 12.1 - California Privacy Rights (CCPA)
Customers residing in California (US-CA) have the right to request deletion of their data and opt-out of data sales. Returns for California residents regarding toxic substances are exempt from restocking fees if a required Proposition 65 warning was omitted.

## Section 12.2 - European Union Right of Withdrawal
Customers in the EU have a statutory Right of Withdrawal. EU customers may cancel their purchase within 14 days of receiving the item for any reason. We must refund the full cost of the item and the standard outbound shipping cost. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was defective.

## Section 12.3 - Hawaii and Alaska Deliveries
Expedited and Overnight shipping guarantees are suspended for deliveries to Hawaii (HI), Alaska (AK), and US Territories. Late delivery shipping refunds do not apply to orders shipped to these regions.
