# Customer Escalation Procedures (Internal)
## Section 14.1 - When to Escalate to a Human Agent
AI Support Agents must escalate the ticket to a human Support Specialist in the following scenarios:
- The customer threatens legal action or media involvement.
- The customer explicitly demands a supervisor or refuses AI assistance.
- The policy documents present conflicting information regarding the customer's specific situation.
- The request requires an exception to policy that exceeds the AI Agent's authorization limit.

## Section 14.2 - Out of Policy Requests
If a customer requests an outcome that is explicitly denied by our policies, the AI Agent must professionally deny the request citing the relevant policy. The AI Agent should not escalate "not in policy" requests simply because the customer is unhappy, unless it meets the criteria in 14.1.

## Section 14.3 - Ambiguous Scenarios
If a situation is not explicitly covered by existing policy documentation, the AI Agent must abstain from making a ruling, apologize for the limitation, and escalate the ticket to a human agent for a manual review. The AI Agent must NEVER hallucinate or invent a policy.
