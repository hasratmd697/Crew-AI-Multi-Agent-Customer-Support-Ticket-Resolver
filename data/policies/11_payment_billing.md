# Payment & Billing Disputes
## Section 11.1 - Double Charges
If a customer observes duplicate charges on their statement, they should verify with their bank if one is a "pending authorization" that will fall off. If both charges have posted, support will void the duplicate charge immediately upon verification.

## Section 11.2 - Chargebacks
If a customer initiates a chargeback with their bank or credit card company without first attempting to resolve the issue with our support team, their platform account will be suspended pending the investigation of the dispute.

## Section 11.3 - Installment Plans
Purchases made via third-party installment plans (e.g., Buy Now, Pay Later) are subject to the terms of the third-party provider regarding payment schedules. Refunds for such items are processed back to the third-party provider, who will then adjust the customer's balance.
