# Electronics Return & Warranty Policy
## Section 3.1 - Standard Electronics Return Window
Electronic items (including computers, tablets, televisions, cameras, and drones) may be returned within 14 days of delivery. The items must be unopened and in the factory-sealed box to be eligible for a full refund.

## Section 3.2 - Opened Electronics & Restocking Fees
If an electronic item has been opened but is otherwise in like-new condition, it may be returned within the 14-day window subject to a 15% restocking fee. Open-box returns must include all original packaging, manuals, cords, and accessories.

## Section 3.3 - Defective Electronics (DOA)
If an electronic item is dead on arrival (DOA) or exhibits a manufacturing defect within the first 14 days, the customer may return it for a full refund or a replacement without any restocking fee. We will cover the return shipping costs.

## Section 3.4 - Manufacturer Warranties
After the 14-day return window, all claims regarding defective electronics must be directed to the manufacturer under their respective warranty policies. We do not process returns or offer technical support for items past the 14-day window.
