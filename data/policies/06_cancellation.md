# Order Cancellation Policy
## Section 6.1 - Cancellation Window
Standard orders can be cancelled for a full refund within 1 hour of placement. After 1 hour, the order enters the fulfillment process and generally cannot be cancelled.

## Section 6.2 - Cancellation of Expedited Orders
Orders placed with expedited or overnight shipping begin processing immediately. The cancellation window for expedited orders is 15 minutes.

## Section 6.3 - Unsuccessful Cancellations
If an order cannot be cancelled because it has already entered shipping workflows, the customer must receive the item and then initiate a standard return (subject to the applicable Return Policy for that item category).

## Section 6.4 - Pre-orders and Backorders
Pre-ordered or backordered items can be cancelled at any time before the item is shipped.
