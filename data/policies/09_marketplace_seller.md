# Marketplace Seller Policy
## Section 9.1 - Seller Fulfilled Return Policies
Items purchased from third-party Marketplace Sellers (indicated by "Sold and Shipped by [Seller Name]") are subject to the individual seller's return policy. Seller return policies must be at least as favorable as our standard 30-day return policy, but processes may vary.

## Section 9.2 - Contacting the Seller
Customers must contact the Marketplace Seller directly for returns, cancellations, or issues with a marketplace order. The seller has 48 business hours to respond to a customer inquiry.

## Section 9.3 - Platform Guarantee
If a Marketplace Seller fails to respond within 48 hours, or refuses to honor their stated return policy, the customer can invoke the Platform Guarantee. We will intervene, and if the seller is found non-compliant, we will issue a full refund directly to the customer.

## Section 9.4 - Restocking Fees for Marketplace Orders
Some marketplace sellers charge restocking fees on non-defective returns. These fees are permitted if explicitly stated on the item listing before purchase.
