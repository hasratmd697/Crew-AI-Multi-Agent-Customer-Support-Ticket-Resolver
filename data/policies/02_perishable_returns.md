# Perishable Items Return Policy
## Section 2.1 - Spoilage & Damage Claims
If a perishable item arrives damaged, spoiled, or otherwise unusable due to shipping delays or poor packaging, customers are eligible for a full refund. Claims must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Customers may keep the damaged items (no physical return required for perishables).

## Section 2.2 - Non-Returnable by Choice
We do not accept returns of perishable items (such as food, flowers, live plants) based on customer preference or "buyer's remorse." Once a perishable order has been prepared or shipped, it cannot be returned or refunded unless there is a verifiable quality issue or spoilage.

## Section 2.3 - Temperature Controlled Shipping
For items requiring temperature control, if the temperature indicator shows that the acceptable temperature was exceeded, a full refund will be issued. 

## Section 2.4 - Refund Process for Perishables
Due to health and safety regulations, do not ship perishable goods back to us. Upon approval of your claim, the refund will be credited back to your original payment method. We may request photographic evidence of the spoiled or damaged goods before authorizing the refund.
