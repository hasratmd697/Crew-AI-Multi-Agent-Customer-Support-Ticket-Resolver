# Lost & Damaged Package Claims
## Section 8.1 - Lost Packages (Marked Delivered)
If tracking indicates a package was delivered but the customer cannot locate it, the customer must wait 48 hours for potential carrier sorting errors to resolve. Afterward, a lost package claim can be filed. Claims must be filed within 14 days of the stated delivery date.

## Section 8.2 - Packages Lost in Transit
If tracking has not updated for 7 consecutive days (14 days for international shipments), the package is considered lost in transit. We will issue a replacement or a full refund immediately upon verifying the tracking standstill.

## Section 8.3 - Items Damaged in Transit
If a package arrives with visible physical damage to the exterior box that results in damage to the internal items, the customer must retain all packaging materials and report the issue within 7 days. Photographic evidence of the box and item damage is required for a refund or replacement.
