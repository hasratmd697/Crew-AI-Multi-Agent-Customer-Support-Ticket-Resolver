# Shipping & Delivery Policy
## Section 7.1 - Shipping Tiers
We offer Standard (5-7 business days), Expedited (2-3 business days), and Overnight shipping. Delivery estimates begin from the date the order ships, not the date the order is placed.

## Section 7.2 - Late Deliveries
If an expedited or overnight order is delivered past its guaranteed delivery date due to carrier delays, we will refund the shipping charges. Standard shipping delivery dates are estimates, and shipping charges are not refunded for minor delays standard shipments.

## Section 7.3 - Refusal of Delivery
If a customer refuses delivery of an order without prior authorization, the return shipping costs will be deducted from the customer's refund once the package is received back at our facility.

## Section 7.4 - Address Errors
We are not responsible for packages that cannot be delivered due to an incorrect or incomplete shipping address provided by the customer. If the package is returned to us as undeliverable, we will issue a refund for the items minus the shipping costs.
