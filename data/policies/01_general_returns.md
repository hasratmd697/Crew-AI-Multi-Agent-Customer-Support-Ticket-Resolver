# General Return & Refund Policy
## Section 1.1 - Return Window
Customers may return most new, unopened items sold and fulfilled by our platform within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The return window begins on the day the item is marked as 'Delivered' by the carrier. 

## Section 1.2 - Condition of Returned Items
Items must be returned in their original condition, with all tags and packaging intact. We reserve the right to deduct a restocking fee of up to 50% for items that are returned damaged, missing parts, not in the original condition, or have obvious signs of use.

## Section 1.3 - Refund Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a full refund when items arrive in a condition that meets our return guidelines. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our fulfillment center. 

## Section 1.4 - Non-Returnable Items
Certain items cannot be returned under this general policy. Please refer to our specific policies regarding Perishable Items, Final Sale Items, and Hygiene & Personal Care Items. Gift cards and downloadable software are also non-returnable and non-refundable.

## Section 1.5 - Return Shipping Costs
If the return is a result of our error (you received an incorrect or defective item, etc.), we will cover the return shipping costs. In all other cases, the customer is responsible for the cost of return shipping.
