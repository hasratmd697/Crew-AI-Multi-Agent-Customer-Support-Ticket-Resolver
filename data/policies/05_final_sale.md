# Final Sale & Clearance Policy
## Section 5.1 - Definition of Final Sale
Items marked as "Final Sale", "Clearance", "Liquidation", or "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase are absolutely not eligible for return, refund, or exchange under any circumstances, barring exceptional delivery issues.

## Section 5.2 - Exceptions for Final Sale
The only exception to the Final Sale policy is if the item arrives materially damaged or if the wrong item was shipped. In such cases, the customer must provide photographic evidence within 7 days of delivery. Upon verification, a refund will be issued.

## Section 5.3 - Price Adjustments
We do not offer price adjustments on previous purchases if those items are subsequently marked down to Final Sale or Clearance pricing.
