# Hygiene & Personal Care Return Policy
## Section 4.1 - Health and Safety Regulations
Due to strict health and safety regulations, we do not accept returns on hygiene, grooming, and personal care products once the protective seal has been broken or the packaging has been opened.

## Section 4.2 - Eligible Hygiene Returns
Unopened hygiene and personal care items in their original, sealed packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

## Section 4.3 - Faulty or Contaminated Products
If a personal care product arrives with a broken seal, leakage, or suspected contamination, the customer must contact support within 3 days of delivery. We will issue a full refund or replacement. Do not use the product. The customer may be asked to dispose of the item rather than returning it.

## Section 4.4 - Applicable Categories
This policy applies to cosmetics, skincare, haircare, oral care products, shaving equipment, fragrances, intimates, and continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) accessories.
